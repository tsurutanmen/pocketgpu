# PocketGPU FAQ and troubleshooting
# PocketGPU よくある質問と、動作しないときの対処表

## English

### Symptom → what to check → how to fix

| Symptom | What to check | How to fix |
|---|---|---|
| The connection drops after streaming for a while over USB | Did it stream continuously for a long time? | **Restart the iPhone first.** This usually fixes it. Change the cable or the USB port only after that. |
| The iPhone app froze after a drop | Is the app stuck on screen? | Swipe the app up to close it, then open it again. |
| The GPU stops being used, or nothing is drawn | Is the iPhone app in front, with the screen on? | Keep the app in front and keep the screen on. If it goes to the background, the GPU cannot be used. |
| `Start-PocketGPU.cmd` stops at a step | It checks in this order: Apple Devices or iTunes, the Apple service, the iPhone over USB, the iPhone app, USB speed, the driver | Do what the message at that step says, then press Enter to check again. If you are stuck, send us `pocketgpu-start-log.txt`. |
| The Apple service is not running | Did you install Apple Devices from the Microsoft Store? | Plug in the iPhone and open Apple Devices once. That starts the service. With iTunes, restart the PC. |
| A game does not see the iPhone GPU | Does the game or its launcher run as administrator? | Windows does not pass the iPhone GPU settings to programs that run as administrator. Start the game without administrator rights. |
| The iPhone app stops opening after about a week | Did you install it with a free Apple ID? | A free Apple ID stops working after 7 days. Install the app again. |
| A DirectX 11 game does not use the iPhone GPU | Are `d3d11.dll` and `dxgi.dll` from `dxvk` next to the game's `.exe`? Did you start the game with `Run-on-iPhone-GPU.cmd` or `pocketgpu.cmd`? | `Run-on-iPhone-GPU.cmd` offers to copy them. By hand: copy the two files from `dxvk\x64` for a 64-bit game or `dxvk\x32` for a 32-bit game next to the `.exe`. |
| The frame rate drops after some time | Is the iPhone hot? | The driver can lower the frame rate when the iPhone gets hot. Cooling with a fan is fine. Do not cool it suddenly, for example with an ice pack. |
| Transfer is slow | `Start-PocketGPU.cmd` step 6 shows the speed. Below about 40 MB/s is USB 2 | Use a USB-C cable that supports USB 3, in a USB-C or USB 3 port on the PC. The cable that comes with the iPhone is USB 2. Only Pro models from the iPhone 15 Pro on support USB 3. |
| A game has anti-cheat | Does the game use Easy Anti-Cheat, BattlEye, VAC or Vanguard? | Do not use these games. Your account may be banned. |

### Questions

**Which games should I not use?**
Games with anti-cheat, such as Easy Anti-Cheat, BattlEye, VAC and Vanguard. Your account may be banned.

**Do I have to keep the app in front?**
Yes. Keep the iPhone app in front with the screen on. The GPU cannot be used when it is in the background.

**Do I need iTunes?**
You need Apple Devices from the Microsoft Store, or iTunes. Windows uses one of them to talk to the iPhone. After installing Apple Devices, plug in the iPhone and open Apple Devices once.

**Why does a game started as administrator not see the iPhone?**
Windows does not pass the iPhone GPU settings to programs that run as administrator. Start the game normally.

**Why does the app stop opening after 7 days?**
A free Apple ID signs the app for 7 days. Install it again.

**Which iPhones are fast over USB?**
Only the Pro models from the iPhone 15 Pro on, with a USB 3 cable and port. Non-Pro models use USB 2 and are slower.

**Can the iPhone get too hot?**
When it is hot, the driver may lower the frame rate. A fan is fine. Do not cool it suddenly, for example with an ice pack.

**How do I send a report?**
"Send logs" in the iPhone app, and on the PC `pocketgpu-start-log.txt` and the file that `report.cmd` writes.

**Not covered here**: anything else, such as what to do if a specific game crashes, or how many apps a free Apple ID can install. Ask us and we will add it.

### 質問

**使わないほうがよいゲームは？**
アンチチートを導入しているゲームです。Easy Anti-Cheat、BattlEye、VAC、Vanguard などがあります。アカウントが停止されるおそれがあります。

**アプリは前面に表示しておく必要がありますか？**
あります。iPhone のアプリを前面に表示して、画面をオンにしたままにしてください。バックグラウンドになると GPU を使えません。

**iTunes は必要ですか？**
Microsoft Store の Apple Devices または iTunes のどちらかが必要です。Windows が iPhone と通信するために使います。Apple Devices をインストールしたら、iPhone を USB ケーブルで接続して、Apple Devices を一度起動してください。

**管理者として起動したゲームが iPhone を認識しないのはなぜですか？**
管理者権限で実行されるアプリには、Windows が iPhone の GPU の設定を渡しません。管理者として実行せずに起動してください。

**なぜ 7 日でアプリが起動できなくなるのですか？**
無料の Apple ID では、アプリへの署名が 7 日で切れるためです。インストールし直してください。

**USB で高速に使える iPhone はどれですか？**
iPhone 15 Pro 以降の Pro モデルを、USB 3 のケーブルとポートで接続したときです。Pro 以外のモデルは USB 2 で遅くなります。

**iPhone が熱くなっても大丈夫ですか？**
iPhone が熱くなると、ドライバがフレームレートを下げることがあります。扇風機で冷やすのはかまいません。保冷剤のように急に冷やすのはやめてください。

**報告はどうやって送りますか？**
iPhone のアプリの「ログを送信」と、PC の `pocketgpu-start-log.txt` と、`report.cmd` が作成するファイルです。

**ここに書いていないこと**：特定のゲームがクラッシュしたときの対処方法、無料の Apple ID でインストールできるアプリの数などです。質問してもらえれば、追加します。
